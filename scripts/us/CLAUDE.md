Publishes a US equity morning brief automatically every trading day. Full history lives in project memory (`us-morning-brief-project`).

## Weekly / monthly recap — US half (design 2026-08-23)

- **Its character is the whole point**: a recap is a **summary of the reports published in that period**, not new reporting — **no web search, no re-collecting quotes.** The primary source is `posts/*.html`, and `scripts/us/recap_source.py` extracts only four things: date, headline, section summary, figures (five posts at 34K tokens each will not fit whole). **The one exception is the period aggregate** — "S&P 500 +1.8% this week" requires multiplying five daily moves, which is agent arithmetic, so the gate blocks it. Hence the split: **prose comes from the posts, the performance table from the aggregate files.**
- **Outputs**: Saturday `/weekly/<YYYY-Www>.html`; the day after month rollover `/monthly/<YYYY-MM>.html`. **The key is the period identifier, not a date** — `period.week_key()` (ISO week) and `month_key()`; the last trading day is `end_date`, a field, never the filename. The CLI does not validate the key format, so a stale `--key 2026-08-21` silently publishes a date URL.
- **Unpublished so far**: `monthly/` does not exist yet (`weekly/` has one). **Before touching a path that has never published, confirm the trigger is actually registered** — the routine file existing is not the same as it being scheduled.
- **Never re-fetch quotes**: aggregates roll up the **closes the posts themselves printed** (`data/history/market.jsonl`), so the last value in the performance table cannot disagree with the last post of the period. Back when it re-collected, it did disagree.
- **Carried-book history**: `data/history/stance.jsonl` and `macro.jsonl` are appended daily. Backfill past entries with `scripts/backfill_history.py`.
- **Scorecard**: `scripts/us/period_scorecard.py` is **a different thing** from `scorecard.py` in main. Only the sign convention is shared (**expanding bonds is a bet on falling yields, so the sign must be flipped for it to count as correct**). Insufficient sample yields `insufficient: true`, and **the gate then requires the post to state that the sample is short**.
- **Gate `scripts/check_period.py`**: the only quotable numbers are the aggregate files and that period's posts. **No day in the period may be missing.** Apply **`mask_dates` first** so dates are not read as figures.
- **Authoring**: `.claude/agents/period-report-writer.md`. **Do not list five summaries — tie them into one narrative.** Review the misses first, and distinguish "the trigger was right but the market did not follow" from "the trigger was designed wrong".
- **Pipeline**: `.claude/WEEKLY_ORCHESTRATOR.md` and `MONTHLY_ORCHESTRATOR.md`. **Most monthly runs must do nothing and exit** — STEP 0 confirms it is not a rollover and stops. The monthly scorecard uses `--spans 3,12 --no-append`.
- **When changing this**: read `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-23-period-reports-design.md` before touching `recap_source.py`, `period_scorecard.py`, or `check_period.py` — the three gate holes, the difference between the two scorecards, and the short-sample rule are there. ## Center of gravity: market/price > judgment/positioning (US body of rule 6 in site/AGENTS.md)

**A gate measures how much of the post is market and price coverage.** Measured 2026-08-27: judgment and positioning 53% against market and price 26%, and the post used 「백분위」, 「최고」, 「평소」 **not once** — even though `price_context` supplies them every day.

- **Price sections open with "where are we now"** — §4, §6, §7 and §8 each start with `<p data-standing="equities|bonds|fx|commodities">` (120+ characters, at least one figure). The material is **position** (percentile and band; do not call it "2 years" when `sessions` < 504), **path** (1 week, 1 month), and **today's size** (`moves.band`) — but **do not fix the order**; lead with whatever there is something to say about that day (fixing it produces the same march four times over). You need not use all three.
- Only assets whose `band` is `큼` or `매우 큼` get a `<p data-cause>` digging into the cause. `미미` and `보통` are exempt — **this is why there is no fixed character-count floor** (demanding 700 characters on a day DXY moved −0.04% is an instruction to pad).
- **Fold §9 on a day that overlaps the previous one** (`abbreviated` in `macro_eval.json`). Folding it into a uniform "same as yesterday" is the same disease as before folding. **If the transmission-block check is not relaxed on folded days, publishing is blocked on most days.**
- **Gate** `scripts/check_weight.py` — ratio (≥0.75 on release days, ≥1.00 on folded days), `data-standing`, `data-cause`, position vocabulary inside price sections, and the §2 `data-lede` order. It also counts narrative table cells (over 40 characters) to block pushing prose into tables. Transmission blocks are compared **directly between the asset section and §9**, not against `market_data` (the post's own rendering diverges from the source value). A bare 「중립」 is not checked.
- **What the gate cannot see**: it checks length, markers and vocabulary but **not the quality of the analysis**. Padding and pro-forma `data-cause` are caught by a human.
