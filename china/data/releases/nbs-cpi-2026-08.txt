Consumer Price Index in August 2026
2026-09-10 09:30
Print |
Large |
Medium |

In August 2026, China’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased by 0.8% year on year. Specifically, the price index in urban areas increased by 0.8% while that in rural areas increased by 0.7%; the price index for food decreased by 1.4% while that for non-food increased by 1.2%; the price index for consumer goods increased by 0.8% while that for services increased by 0.8%. From January to August, on average, China’s CPI increased by 0.9% year on year.   In August, China’s CPI increased by 0.4% month on month. Specifically, the price index in urban areas increased by 0.4% while that in rural areas increased by 0.4%; the price index for food increased by 0.4% while that for non-food increased by 0.3%; the price index for consumer goods increased by 0.6% while that for services increased by 0.1%.     I. Year-on-Year Changes in Price Indexes for Various Goods and Services   In August, the price index for food, tobacco, liquor and dining out decreased by 0.7% year on year, bringing the CPI down by about 0.21 percentage points. Among food, the price index for meat of livestock decreased by 5.1%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.21 percentage points, of which the price index for pork decreased by 11.8%; that for fresh vegetables decreased by 2.8%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.05 percentage points; that for milk and other dairy products decreased by 1.4%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.01 percentage points; that for aquatic products decreased by 0.6%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.01 percentage points; that for eggs increased by 15.0%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.07 percentage points.   Among the price indexes for the other seven major categories, six increased, and one decreased year on year. Specifically, the price indexes for miscellaneous goods and services, health care and medical services, and transportation and telecommunication increased by 7.3%, 2.7% and 2.5%, respectively; the price indexes for education, culture and recreation, clothing and household facilities, articles and services increased by 1.4%, 1.3% and 0.7%, respectively; and the price index for residence decreased by 0.3%.     II. Month-on-Month Changes in Price Indexes for Various Goods and Services   In August, the price index for food, tobacco, liquor and dining out increased by 0.3% month on month, pushing the CPI up by about 0.07 percentage points. Among food, the price index for fresh vegetables increased by 5.5%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.09 percentage points; that for eggs increased by 2.0%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.01 percentage points; that for meat of livestock increased by 0.9%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.03 percentage points, of which the price index for pork increased by 1.3%; that for fresh fruits decreased by 2.5%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.05 percentage points; that for aquatic products decreased by 0.9%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.02 percentage points.   Among the price indexes for the other seven major categories, two increased, three remained flat and two decreased month on month. Specifically, the price indexes for transportation and telecommunication, miscellaneous goods and services increased by 1.7% and 1.5%, respectively; the price indexes for residence, education, culture and recreation, and health care and medical services all remained flat; the price indexes for household facilities, articles and services and clothing decreased by 0.2% and 0.1%, respectively.     Consumer Price Indexes in August 2026

10.3   Annotations:   1. Index Interpretation   The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is an indicator that measures relative changes in the price level of consumer goods and services over time. It comprehensively reflects changes in prices of consumer goods and services purchased by residents.   2. Statistical Coverage   The CPI statistical survey covers the prices of eight major categories of goods and services purchased by national urban and rural residents, including food, tobacco, liquor and dining out, clothing, residence, household facilities, articles and services, transportation and telecommunication, education, culture and recreation, health care and medical services, and miscellaneous goods and services, as well as 268 basic categories.   3. Survey Method   Survey sites are selected using a sampling method. In accordance with the principle of “fixed person, fixed site, and fixed time”, original prices are collected either through on-site visits to the survey sites or from online sources. The data comes from nearly 120,000 survey sites in about 500 cities and counties in 31 provinces (autonomous regions and municipalities), covering shopping malls (stores), supermarkets, farmers’ (fresh produce) markets, service outlets, and Internet e-commerce.   4. Data Description   Due to rounding, the aggregate data may be equal to the high or low value of the classified figures.   5. Base Period Rotation   According to the statistical system arrangement, starting from January 2026, China’s CPI has been compiled and published with 2025 as the base period. Compared with the previous base period, the survey categories, representative specification items, surveyed sites, and the category weights have been adjusted to reflect the latest changes in the consumption structure. According to calculations, the impact of the base period rotation on the monthly year-on-year CPI is approximately 0.06 percentage points on average.
